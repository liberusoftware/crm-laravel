# v13.0.0 — CRM workspaces, auto dialing and role management

## CRM usability

- Organize contact, lead and deal forms into clear sections, with optional advanced details.
- Remember table searches, filters and sorting, and provide clearer statuses and empty states.
- Identify leads by their linked contact, show expected close dates and add deal pipeline/stage filters.
- Correct lead-score filtering when the minimum or maximum is zero.

## Calling and outreach

- Call contacts directly from contact and lead screens using configured team Twilio credentials.
- Queue selected contacts for sequential auto dialing, with pause/resume controls and remembered agent numbers.
- Filter contacts and leads by never called, not called today, or not called in the last 7 or 14 days.
- Recheck eligibility before dispatch and avoid duplicate active calls, recently called contacts and opted-out numbers.
- Record call-attempt timestamps and preserve uncertain provider responses for status reconciliation.

## Roles and permissions

- Build and edit team roles with readable permission labels, search and selection summaries.
- Start from Sales, Support, Social media or Advertising presets, then customize the selected permissions.
- Restrict global role configuration to super administrators and protect system role names and the super-administrator role.
- Enforce current-team authorization, a reviewed grantable-permission catalog and case-insensitive reserved names on the server.
- Replace team-role grants without retaining the previous custom role or affecting another team's assignments.
- Keep role changes and audit records transactional, and preserve verified invitation, SSO and SAML provisioning.

## Upgrade notes

- Run `composer install`, then `php artisan migrate` to add call-attempt tracking and history indexes.
- Keep the Laravel scheduler running for auto dialing, and configure a public application URL for Twilio status callbacks.
- Review existing custom roles against the grantable business-permission catalog. Unknown or privileged permissions are rejected, and reserved system names cannot be used for team roles.
- Reassign members before deleting an assigned role. Global system-role definitions affect every team using them.

See [calling setup](../contact-dialing.md) and [role management](../role-permissions.md) for usage and operating details.
